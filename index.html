How the stages build up

The alphabet comes in five small groups (A–E, F–J, K–O, P–T, U–Z). Each group has four stages, and from the second group on a review mixes in everything learned so far.

  1. Meet the letters — big and little letter, its sound and a picture word ("A says /a/, like apple"). Then she finds the picture. Tapping the letter friend says the sound again.
  2. Trace — big letter, then little letter, stroke by stroke. The next dot to reach pulses orange and the yellow dot shows where each stroke starts. It only finishes when the whole letter is traced in order, so half a letter never counts. A small Skip button is there for you.
  3. Big and little — match upper and lower case. Look-alikes (b d p q, n u, m w) turn up as distractors.
  4. Letter sounds — which letter makes this sound, what a word starts with, and which picture starts with a sound. Letters that share a sound (c and k) are never offered together.
  5. Review — a mix of all the letters so far, leaning on the ones she found hard.
  6. ABC order — the missing letter in a row of four, and what comes before or after.
  7. First words — three letter tiles (c-a-t) she can tap to hear each sound, then she blends them and picks the picture.
  8. Big mix — a little of everything.

The ABC song button on the home screen sings through the letters with a bouncing highlight; tapping any letter there says its sound and picture word.

How the game behaves

  • Five or six questions per stage. Short is better than long.
  • A wrong answer greys out and cannot be tapped again in that question, so she cannot guess her way through.
  • There is never a "wrong" screen — only a gentle "try again".
  • After two tries the right answer gently pulses.
  • Two stars or more opens the next stage. A one-star finish stays open so she can play it again.
  • The game says letter sounds (/b/ as "buh") as well as letter names, which is how phonics is taught in school.

Her name

Voice

The game speaks with a warm, natural recorded voice that works offline. You can switch to one of your phone's own voices here if you prefer.

This browser has no speech engine, so the game will be silent. The words still appear on screen. Opening the game in Chrome or Safari usually brings the voice back.

The whole course

Every stage in order. Play now jumps straight in; Unlock opens a stage without her having to earn it.
